My priority date

Cutoff date by bulletin

X axis: bulletin month. Y axis: the cutoff priority date in that bulletin. Steeper means faster movement; a drop is a retrogression. "C" (current) is plotted at the bulletin month itself.

Wait in line (bulletin month − cutoff)

A backlog proxy: roughly how many years a case filed today would wait. A rising line means the cutoff is moving slower than the calendar and the backlog is growing.

Months advanced per year

Each October bulletin (start of the fiscal year) compared with the previous October. 12 months means keeping pace with the calendar; negative values are retrogressions.

When USCIS accepted Chart B (Dates for Filing)

Each month USCIS says which Visa Bulletin chart employment-based I-485 filers must use. B: Dates for Filing (earlier cutoffs, you can file sooner). A: Final Action Dates. Rows are fiscal years, October to September.

Monthly data table

Projections come from the visa-bulletin.us queue model, not from the State Department. Blank values are treated as Current (C); a few months may actually have been Unavailable (U).
